A custom business tool

Time cards without the paperwork.

This private web app gives a crew a quick way to record hours while giving the owner a reliable view of labor by person, job, and day.

How the workflow works

  • Employees sign in and log the time they worked.
  • They choose the specific job or project.
  • The app totals each day and keeps the owner’s overview organized.
  • The admin dashboard highlights crew totals and entries that need review.
  • Names and private information are intentionally hidden in this public preview.
